SWA Formula

SWA = Σ (Marks × Credits) ÷ Σ Credits

Each course mark is multiplied by its credit value. The sum of all weighted marks is divided by the total number of credits. Only marks recorded for the selected semester and academic year count; course registration alone is not enough to calculate SWA.
